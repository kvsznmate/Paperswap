Privacy

PaperSwap · last changed 18 November 2026

PaperSwap has no accounts, no ads, no tracking cookies and no third-party analytics. The server does not store your IP address or anything else that names you.

PaperSwap is a non-commercial demo project by a student, published at github.com/kvsznmate/Paperswap. The maintainer of that repository is responsible for the data described here. To ask a question or use one of your rights, open an issue there.

What the server stores

Every row below is tied at most to a random id that your browser or app made up. Nothing links that id to you.

The articles themselves (headlines, descriptions, links) are public publisher data, kept for {{ARTICLE_DAYS}} days.

What the server only holds in memory

  • Your IP address, as a key in the rate limiter that stops one client from flooding the server. It is never written to the database or to a log file, and it is gone after the limit window (at most one hour) or when the server restarts.
  • The last 100 requests (method, path and status, no address), for the operator's status screen.

What stays on your device

  • Web page: your saved articles, your topic choice and the random session id, in this browser's local storage. Clear the site data to remove them.
  • Android app: your saved articles and topic choice, in the app's storage. Clear the app's storage or uninstall it to remove them. The app makes a new session id each time it starts and does not keep it.

Other servers your device contacts

  • Publishers' image servers. Card photos load straight from the publisher (for example the BBC or Ars Technica), so that publisher sees your IP address, under its own privacy policy. Cards without a photo contact no one.
  • The publisher's site, when you open an article.

Fonts are served by PaperSwap itself, not by Google. The server runs on Oracle Cloud Infrastructure, which hosts the data on the project's behalf. The connection is plain HTTP for now; HTTPS is planned (#32).

Legal basis

Legitimate interest (GDPR Article 6(1)(f)): running the service, protecting it from abuse, and measuring how the demo is used. The data is minimal and cannot identify you on its own.

Your rights

You can ask for access to, or deletion of, your data, and you can object to its use. Because PaperSwap holds nothing that identifies you, it can only find your rows if you give it your session id (GDPR Article 11). You can also complain to the Dutch data protection authority, the Autoriteit Persoonsgegevens.
